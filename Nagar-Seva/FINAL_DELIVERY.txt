================================================================================
                          API ENDPOINTS READY
================================================================================

POST /api/complaints
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Request:  {category, description, location, latitude, longitude, photoData}
Response: 201 Created {id, category, description, ..., status: "OPEN"}

GET /api/complaints
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Response: 200 OK [{id, category, ...}, ...]

GET /api/complaints/{id}
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Response: 200 OK {id, category, ...} OR 404 Not Found

PUT /api/complaints/{id}
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Request:  Partial complaint object to update
Response: 200 OK {updated complaint} OR 404

PATCH /api/complaints/{id}/status ⭐
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Request:  {status: "OPEN|IN_PROGRESS|RESOLVED"}
Response: 200 OK {updated complaint} OR 404

DELETE /api/complaints/{id}
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Response: 204 No Content OR 404
